<div data-lang="en">
    The smallest yellow ball with blue strike on it is a bouncing ball; the medium purple ball with gray strike acts like a bowling ball; the largest green ball with orange strike is simulating a beach ball. By implementing <b>The Twelve Basic Principles of Animation</b>, such like "slow in and slow out", "arcs" and "timing", the ball looks not only real but also more appealing by its cartooning style. Also, "squash and stretch" makes the bouncing ball lifelike because of its resilience.<br>
